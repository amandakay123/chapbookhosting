These Days I Grow More and More Insomniac

So Benadryl every night this week and I learn sleep is the natural forgetting of my heart, overcaffeinated. days, calendared. I've perfected the secret to remaining young, flew paper planes out the window, their wings refracting into light. in the absence of dreams, I cannot afford my lifestyle, to wonder of life beyond my work, once again, the hours I spent not knowing how I'll make rent. waking despite the night, swallowing my cries with fistfuls of sheep. I'm almost at that hallucinatory stage, a scattering of ash. some natural disaster dissipating like rain against window. childhood an eroding force, forgetting my stuffed dog I'm kinda losing things all the time, you know keys, slipping through the sinkhole of Market St. it's not my fault I no longer believe despite this, I am dedicated to finding someone you don't know. wallow in this lullaby, some fairytale divination, grape-syrup thumbs folding paper to sleep. I know about my bad habits, circular smoke. a garden pruned of leaves, months without water. my reflection ripples across the years, still what good is an unconscious life
the difference between sex and the body: the hours spent pulsing, David on the 29th, his friend the day after, silent, wondering if he likes his girls stupid or too tired to speak. moths fuck near neon signs, of life open late, greasy burgers, cans of Monster, fries. this is dangerous— the urge to be full, proof of existence: lying on my back, sweat sticking to bedsheet I want this to remain a love poem to my favorite boy, waiting in the foyer. twenty-one, aged out of gullibility, cigarette smoke no longer melodrama, now a name scrawled in charcoal. my future self always dying. last night's prescription on the nightstand, found artsy ways to say this is all I have: a beautiful body, a pair of hands. make everything romantic: night near a brilliant dawn, what crests over horizon? one more mojito please. extra shot. let me be teenage angst a little longer. let me sing of summer lingering through sleep. this is me: translating heat, some ill-fated gravitation, how swiftly the hands carry the weight. chiseling away the excess, I am left with the image of myself made mirrorless, if not to preserve the view from above
