Open Letter to My Machine Made Self

I just wanted to say, that for once, the years Having not done my hurting properly, forgoes circuitry. That, even in this body, I, made of moving parts, have refused the science Of rust. Leftover SpaghettiOs. Broken teeth. Today, I want you to pick up every call that goes Ignored. Remember that not all love comes constant. Today, I am proud that I am so full of sky. Dead engines. Hoarding America's abandoned satellites and space trash. Like I'm hungry for it. Here's the time to treasure Everything that glistens, your father's old cassettes, your grandfather's voice, knowing I, too, will become obsolete. There's enough ways to forget Without you adding to it. Things come and go like sentences. Still, I love you, even when you fail to remember The difference between light and love, ways to prove I am empty, everything moves in the shape of sun Circling like ravens over a dying bull. That even after this life I am still left with an open pasture, these silly little horns, Places where I started out beautiful
