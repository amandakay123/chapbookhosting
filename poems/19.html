Moving Forward

every day, moving forward. watching the news. cataloguing your grief. starting and ending the day staring at a different face. tired of routine, I've decided to blow it all up. choosing the next best thing, scrolling on Tinder a whole supermarket's worth of men. on Sunday, I told my dad I couldn't do this anymore, that I'd die if I tried, took the train back home. remember its strange rattle as the ground shook, some hurt unfurling within you, like the butt of an unknown root. maybe a tuber. point is, sometimes the quake reveals the buried to the air. this time, it jams it deeper into the earth. I continue to scroll. these days, I hear nothing but the echoes reverberating through the earth. tryna match the footprints. you keep moving forward, trudging through the damp, the mud clinging to the crevices of your boots. there's only so long you can walk until it comes back to you. all that dirt, all that grass, the pollen of your front-yard tree, coming to rest in your hair.
