disillusionment

whatever it was I would've said had it not been for the pearl or the fake opal of its imitation, whatever it was I would've said instead about the heart instead of the metal ripping flesh. whatever it was I would've said had it been about the sweetness of music. today, I name my favorite poets and watch the world rearrange the cadence of its words, output a permutation. I can give you different ways to describe it, this academic variation on grief–vases without water, the sun against powerlines, a shopping cart in a river. anything can be automated if done well enough, including love. we tell you that it can write in the style of anything if it tries hard enough, gives you at least this. but what are these images if not leaves plucked from a now dying tree? to turn our face from the axe's swish and cull, plastic air freshener mimicking sequoia in some final breeze.
