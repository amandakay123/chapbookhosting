Love Poem With Goodbyes

making each day better than the last. listening to the music when the rain comes in. days, devoid of alcohol and lust. days, full of apologies, then leaving. dear reader, let me embrace every cliche. I want a life that is so full of sunlight. the smell of fresh laundry. of huddling under the covers. in the garden, I would plant something mundane. like carrots. or marigolds. I would no longer write despite there being so much to write about. there exists a love that hurts and then a love that fades. the world aches and hungers. this is a poem, just like any poem, but it closes.
