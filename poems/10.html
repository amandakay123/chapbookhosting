sober

let there be a disco ball and let there be a dance floor. let there be a boy you love and a boy you envy. I wanted to chug from his bottle, to wear his skin like a coat. I wanted to be lovable the way feet can learn to love a road, if not for all those bright lights, all that chloroform. reality is, there is so much of the world I have left to touch except I spend it in the same dark rooms. everything novel about the world leaves you, dying in small corners. truth is, I walk into the world hungover and it shows. I litter the world with gasps and beer cans. even now, I can hear the music of it, the clash of metal against concrete. glass shatters, I cannot tell if it is my phone or if it's my hands.
