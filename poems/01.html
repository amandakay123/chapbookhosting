I wonder how I'll find New Love…

After "In the Flesh" by Ecco2k

I begin my days flinching from the cold. my lover sits by candlelight, turning worn pages, he's a man of the word. his word. and vintage! thrifts his sweater vests, only buys organic. he's a good body, especially in comparison to the others. dime-a-dozen at the supermarket. we are in such a nourishing time. this century of mechanical revolution. the wheels turn out of your grasp. the feeling comes and leaves through the doorway. you burn your tongue while drinking tea. you can no longer taste the bitter. thank god for the numbness. the reversal of frostbite. every act of extreme weather. the creatures in the dirt, wriggling towards the sky.

I tell my lover that I want to write a good poem. first, you have to be an alive girl. the light dims from his breath. frost stroking the window. call it fog inside and out. call it summer leaving the body of spring. heartbeat flickering, like candlelight. his ear, on my chest. I want to write without words. love is the currency of my body. his ear, in my mouth. winter, the exchange of breaths. making of love as sure as a storm. my lover touches my cyborgean body, flinches from the cold. the months like a turnstile. he enters me, then passes. at university, I am tested on foreign numbers and language. look, I have so much metal around me. a year of snakes. skin shedding tongue. my lover's teeth breaking the barrier. we fucked through the sirens outside. we fucked through the month of his father's death. I did not register the heat of his skin. couldn't tell the difference between his sighs and his silence.

at dusk, I steal windchimes. leave fur on the neighbor's porch. I am this new century's tooth fairy. my incisors, some special delight. swallowing the ulterior motive. sparrow strangled midflight. this old ground grows parched, forgets the lingering of snow. still the powder on your breath. still the century of lust. grief is like fucking but more pornographic. my lover livestreams his father's funeral. of procession. of progression. I learn to iron out the kinks. my lover, an intellectual, studies my brain. I record my psychiatrist's diagnosis. the tape loops on the VHS. his hands, cold, find the tufts of my hair. my lover knows I'll immortalize his brain. made permanent in the pixels. splayed white on screen. his orgasm leftover in my hair.

california loses to the smoke's lust. my father's hair greys every day. I tell my lover this and he grows green with envy. the most green thing around. for miles I see nothing but afterthoughts. a masterclass in counting down, moving forward, swallowing. I rename my grief in my lover's bedsheets. call it god or some other animal. call it blood or some other fluid. tea stains the linen, bitters his mouth. his swallow dies midflight. his gasp in my chest. during sex, a heartbeat or a siren. people make space when I enter the room. my lover only fucks me in the dark.

with the moon, every month I bleed. I claim ownership of my metal, my ovaries. the ozone my hymen. every thrust, like light. I bleed through the atmosphere. my pelvis wide and bowl-like, rumbles like an engine. heat fills the empty space. I know how to tell the time from the movement of the sky. a car backfires, releases smoke. my mouth opens in anticipation. that is how the song ends.

america is my metal. my lover, who becomes not my lover, on a random thursday, sings. light flinches from me, this steel body. I want to be a better body of wax. to let light and heat escape. I want to be an alive girl. to collect coins on the side of the road. to shed my teeth like the skin of a cottonmouth. I want to find New Love in the pocket of a trenchcoat in a thrift store somewhere. in a leaflet telling me to find god. god, who comes as an apparition, loiters. I move forward through the murk. my sadness like fog. sleep, my new lover, becomes the pressure on my chest, the dirt in my lungs. I forage for new light. a swallow dies in midflight. an engine rumbles, a soundtrack to the end.
